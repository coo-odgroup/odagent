CANCELLATION POLICY

The tickets booked through ODBUS can be cancelled before 12 hours of departure of the said bus.

  1. Partial cancellation is not allowed in ODBUS.
  2. For refund issues mail support@odbus.in mentioning PNR.
  3. Refunds are normally processed within 10-12 working days.
  4. Customer can't claim cancellation refund on rescheduled tickets.
  5. Cancellation terms vary according to operator policy.
  6. Tickets booked online should be cancelled online only.
  7. Transaction Fee / GST are non-refundable.
  8. Cancellation charges apply on Base Fare only.
