1.1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ODBUS ("ODBUS," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information in connection with the ODBUS Operator Mobile Application.

The App is provided exclusively to authorized bus operators who are registered partners of ODBUS.

By accessing or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy applies only to the App and does not apply to other ODBUS websites, applications, or services, which may have separate privacy policies.

1.2 Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information
    Login credentials, operator ID, contact number, and business details.
  • Operational Data
    Seat management, fare change requests, cancellation requests, and other actions.
  • Booking Related Data
    Booking and cancellation information relevant to your services.
  • Device & Technical Information
    Device type, operating system, IP address, device identifiers, application version, and crash logs.
  • Usage Data
    Login timestamps, features used, and session duration.

We collect only the information necessary to operate and improve the App.

1.3 How We Use Information

  • Authenticate operators securely.
  • Provide booking and seat management features.
  • Maintain accurate inventory.
  • Provide operator support.
  • Prevent fraud and misuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Communicate service updates.

We never sell operator data or use it for third-party advertising.

1.4 User Accounts

Access requires an operator account issued by ODBUS. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials and must notify ODBUS immediately if unauthorized access is suspected.

1.5 Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards including encrypted communication, secure infrastructure, and access controls.

While we strive to protect your information, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

1.6 Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party providers such as cloud hosting, analytics, and crash reporting services solely to support App functionality.

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and cannot use it for independent purposes.

1.7 Data Retention

Operator account information is retained while your account remains active and for a reasonable period thereafter to comply with legal and operational requirements.

1.8 Operator Responsibilities

  • Ensure submitted information is accurate.
  • Use the App only for legitimate operational purposes.
  • Protect login credentials.
  • Report unauthorized access or suspected breaches immediately.

1.9 Children's Privacy

The App is intended only for authorized adult bus operators and their staff. It is not intended for individuals under the age of 18.

1.10 Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, legal obligations, or operational practices.
