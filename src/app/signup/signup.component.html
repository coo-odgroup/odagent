FAQs Related to Bus Tickets Booking

An e-ticket, also known as an electronic ticket, is an electronic document that contains the details of your bus ticket, like a unique confirmation number issued by the bus operator for the booking you have made, along with other details like your departure date, time, boarding point, conductor number and so on. It is important to carry a print-out of the e-ticket along with a valid photo-id proof at the time of bus departure.
ODBUS provides access to a wide range of bus operators, convenient online booking, secure payments and easy ticket management.
You can create an account with ODBUS to manage your bookings and access your ticket information conveniently.
Online booking allows you to conveniently compare buses, routes and fares before completing your booking.
Multi-city booking availability depends on the routes and operators available on ODBUS.
Please check the applicable operator rules and requirements before travelling with an infant.
